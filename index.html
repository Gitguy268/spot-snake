SPOT · AGE 2 SCORE 000 BEST 000

BISCUIT TIME

Lick biscuits to grow. Gold ones score 5. Mind the fence, the flowers and your tail.

Arrows, WASD or a gamepad steer. Space pauses. M changes the sound. On a phone, swipe on the lawn or use the pad. Spot speeds up as the biscuits add up.

Built on ideas from epidemian/snake and janbodnar/Java-Snake-Game.
